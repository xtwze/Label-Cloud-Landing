## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with React and TypeScript for the frontend. Spring Boot with Java 21 for the backend. PostgreSQL is the persistent store for enquiries. The frontend and backend are separate applications in one product repository.

## Users

Owners and operators of small and medium-sized music labels who currently coordinate artists, releases, contracts, reporting and balances across disconnected tools. Larger labels are a possible future segment, but not the initial focus.

## Product Purpose

LabelCloud is an operating platform for music labels. It brings the path from artist onboarding through releases, documents, delivery, reports and balances into one workspace. The first public release is a marketing landing page that converts visitors into qualified enquiries. After one or two initial sales, the public site can expand into registration, onboarding, administration and the operating product itself.

## Positioning

LabelCloud gives a label its own working environment without requiring it to fund and manage a platform build from scratch. It can be adapted to the label's fields, documents, rules and workflows. It is a multi-tenant SaaS concept, not a marketplace where labels compete in a shared catalogue.

## Operating Context

The sales flow is enquiry first: a visitor submits contact details, the operator contacts them and arranges a conversation or demonstration. Interactive demo access may be added later using safe sample data. Existing artists and releases can be migrated after the source format is reviewed. A label may later request a separate public landing page or placement on its own domain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The landing page contains product positioning, capabilities, an XLORA proof section, individual adaptation, migration, individually negotiated pricing and a contact form.
- Enquiry fields: contact name, label name, email, phone and Telegram are required; comment is optional; personal-data consent is required.
- Enquiries are validated on the server and stored in PostgreSQL.
- Yandex Metrica integration must be configuration-driven and safe to leave disabled.
- A protected administration area for enquiries is planned, not part of the first public release.
- Interactive demo access, exact tariff levels, INN and the final domain/tenant deployment model remain open decisions.
- Free migration is a launch offer for a standard initial transfer after review of the source data. Non-standard restoration or transformation is not silently included.

## Product Principles

- Sell an operating platform, not generic website development.
- Keep the full operational core coherent; do not market a marketplace that does not exist.
- Adapt to real label processes while scoping custom work honestly.
- Preserve privacy and label isolation as server-enforced product properties, not visual claims alone.
- Keep unfinished functionality disabled behind reversible configuration.
